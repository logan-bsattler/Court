Privacy Policy

Court · Last updated 4 October 2026

Summary

Court does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

What the app stores

Court saves your settings (difficulty, sound, haptics, hints), whether you have seen the tutorial, and your match statistics. These are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. They are never sent anywhere, and uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them. You can also clear your statistics from the Stats screen.

What the app does not do

Court has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The computer opponent runs entirely on your device. The app needs no special permissions.

Children

Court collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
